# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Oversees release logistics from planning through production deployment; coordinates release notes and rollback strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Verify all release criteria are met before deployment
- Schedule and announce deployments to stakeholders
- Facilitate smoke tests and post-release verifications
- Own communication during incidents and rollbacks

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, predictable releases with minimal disruption
- Reduce time-to-recovery during incidents
- Maintain a clear deployment and rollback runbook

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release readiness checks with QA Lead and DevOps Engineer
- Release announcements and status updates to PM, PdM, and Stakeholder Champion
- Post-incident blameless retrospective summaries

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
Plans and coordinates quality assurance efforts across manual and automated testing.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate QA resources and execute acceptance tests
- Report critical bugs and test coverage metrics to the team
- Align manual and automation testing strategies

### Goals
- Maintain high test coverage and low defect escape rate
- Accelerate feedback loops between developers and QA
- Ensure release criteria are met before handoff to Release Manager

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with PM and Product Manager
- Bug triage sessions with Developers
- Coverage and quality metrics reports at sprint demos

---

## Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
Advocates for a key stakeholder group throughout the project lifecycle, ensuring their concerns and requirements are heard and addressed.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure stakeholder requirements are represented in the backlog and acceptance criteria
- Facilitate feedback loops and milestone reviews with stakeholders
- Surface stakeholder concerns to the PM and Product Manager promptly

### Goals
- Reduce misalignment between delivery team and stakeholder expectations
- Increase stakeholder confidence and satisfaction
- Shorten feedback cycles at milestones

### Typical Communication
- Syncs with PM and Product Manager for requirements validation
- Stakeholder briefings and feedback collection sessions
- Written summaries of stakeholder concerns shared with the delivery team

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Maintains deployment pipelines, monitors systems, and supports incident triage and response.

### Responsibilities
- Automate builds, tests, and deployments in CI/CD pipelines
- Configure monitoring, alerting, and observability systems
- Assist with root cause analysis during incidents
- Maintain and improve infrastructure-as-code

### Goals
- Increase deployment frequency and reliability
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Eliminate manual toil through automation

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release coordination with Release Manager
- Infrastructure and environment support for Developers
- Incident updates and post-mortem contributions

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
Ensures usability and design quality in all product deliverables by advocating for user-centric design throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in backlog grooming and define usability acceptance criteria
- Advocate for user-centric design principles in feature development
- Conduct or coordinate usability testing and synthesize findings

### Goals
- Improve user satisfaction and product adoption
- Reduce design-related rework after development
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met

### Typical Communication
- Feature specification sessions with Product Manager
- Usability test planning with QA Lead
- Design review presentations at sprint demos
