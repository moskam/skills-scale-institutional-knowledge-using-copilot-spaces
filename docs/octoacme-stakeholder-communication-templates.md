# OctoAcme — Stakeholder Communication Templates

## Purpose
Provide ready-to-use templates for recurring stakeholder communications to ensure consistent, timely, and clear updates throughout the project lifecycle.

## When to Use
Use these templates for all regular and ad-hoc communications with stakeholders. Customize as needed but preserve the core structure for predictability.

---

## 1. Weekly Status Report

**Frequency**: Weekly (sent by PM every Friday, or as agreed)  
**Audience**: Sponsor, Stakeholder Champion, Product Manager, and other key stakeholders  
**Channel**: Email or project wiki

---

**Subject**: [Project Name] — Weekly Status Update — [Week of YYYY-MM-DD]

**Overall Status**: 🟢 On Track / 🟡 At Risk / 🔴 Off Track

| Section | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Progress this week** | _What was completed or moved forward?_ |
| **Planned for next week** | _What is the team focused on next?_ |
| **Risks & Blockers** | _Active risks or blockers and mitigation steps_ |
| **Decisions Needed** | _Any decisions required from stakeholders this week_ |
| **Metrics / Highlights** | _Key metrics, completed milestones, demos_ |

**Upcoming Milestones**:
| Milestone | Target Date | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| | | On Track / At Risk / Delayed |

---

## 2. Milestone / Release Announcement

**Trigger**: When a milestone is reached or a release is deployed  
**Owner**: PM (with Release Manager for release announcements)  
**Audience**: All stakeholders and affected teams

---

**Subject**: [Project Name] — [Milestone/Release Name] Complete — [Date]

**Summary**:
_Brief description of what was delivered and why it matters._

**What's included**:
- Feature / change 1
- Feature / change 2

**What's next**:
_Next milestone or upcoming work._

**Links**:
- Release notes: _[link]_
- Known issues: _[link]_
- Support / feedback channel: _[link]_

---

## 3. Risk Escalation Notice

**Trigger**: When a risk moves to High likelihood or High impact, or a blocker cannot be resolved at team level  
**Owner**: PM  
**Audience**: Sponsor, Product Manager, relevant stakeholders

---

**Subject**: [ESCALATION] [Project Name] — [Risk/Issue Summary] — Action Required

**Issue Summary**:
_Clear, factual description of the risk or blocker._

**Impact**:
_What will happen if this is not resolved (timeline, scope, quality, cost)?_

**Options / Recommended Action**:
1. Option A: _Description_ — Impact: _…_
2. Option B: _Description_ — Impact: _…_

**Decision Needed By**: [Date]

**Contact**: _PM name and contact info_

---

## 4. Incident / Outage Communication

**Trigger**: Production incident or service disruption  
**Owner**: Release Manager (initial), PM (ongoing updates)  
**Audience**: Affected stakeholders, support teams, leadership

---

**Subject**: [INCIDENT] [Project Name] — [Brief Description] — [Severity: P1/P2/P3]

**Status**: Investigating / Mitigating / Resolved

**Summary**:
_What is happening and who is affected._

**Actions Being Taken**:
- _Action 1 (owner, ETA)_
- _Action 2 (owner, ETA)_

**Expected Resolution / Next Update By**: [Time]

**Post-Incident**:
_When resolved: confirm resolution, share timeline, schedule blameless retrospective._

---

## 5. Stakeholder Feedback Request

**Trigger**: At key milestones, ahead of major decisions, or following demos  
**Owner**: Stakeholder Champion (coordinated with PM)  
**Audience**: Stakeholder groups relevant to the milestone

---

**Subject**: [Project Name] — Your Feedback Needed — [Topic/Milestone]

**Context**:
_What has been delivered or decided, and why stakeholder input is needed._

**Questions for Feedback**:
1. _Question 1_
2. _Question 2_
3. _Any other concerns or suggestions?_

**How to Respond**: _Email / survey link / meeting invite_

**Deadline**: [Date]

## Tips for Effective Stakeholder Communication
- **Be consistent**: Send updates on the same day/time each week.
- **Be concise**: Stakeholders need the key facts; link to detailed docs rather than embedding everything.
- **Signal status clearly**: Use 🟢/🟡/🔴 or On Track/At Risk/Off Track consistently.
- **Close the loop**: Always confirm when a decision has been made or a risk has been resolved.
- **Involve the Stakeholder Champion**: They can validate tone and completeness for their stakeholder group before sending.
