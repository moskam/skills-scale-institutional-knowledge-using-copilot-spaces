# OctoAcme — Project Kickoff Template

## Purpose
Provide a structured agenda and checklist to ensure every project kickoff meeting covers the essential topics, aligns all participants on goals and responsibilities, and captures decisions and action items.

## When to Use
Run this template at the kickoff meeting following approval of the Project One-pager (see [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md)). All core team members and key stakeholders should attend.

---

## Kickoff Meeting Agenda

| # | Topic | Owner | Time |
|---|-------|-------|------|
| 1 | Welcome and introductions | PM | 5 min |
| 2 | Project background and business case | Product Manager (PdM) | 10 min |
| 3 | Goals, success metrics, and scope | PdM + PM | 10 min |
| 4 | Team roles and responsibilities | PM | 10 min |
| 5 | High-level timeline and milestones | PM | 10 min |
| 6 | Process overview (planning, tracking, release) | PM | 5 min |
| 7 | Known risks and dependencies | PM + Team | 10 min |
| 8 | Open Q&A and action items | All | 10 min |

## Post-Kickoff Checklist
- [ ] Meeting notes and decisions shared with all attendees
- [ ] Action items logged in project board
- [ ] Project board set up (columns: Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done)
- [ ] Backlog grooming session scheduled
- [ ] Communication cadence and channels agreed (standup time, status report format)
- [ ] Risk register created and initial risks logged
- [ ] Stakeholder Champion confirmed and stakeholder list finalized
- [ ] QA Lead has received the initial acceptance criteria
- [ ] DevOps Engineer briefed on infrastructure and CI/CD requirements
- [ ] UX Designer included in backlog grooming for user-facing items
